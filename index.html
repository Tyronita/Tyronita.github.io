Text2WetLab

🏆 Anthropic Science Hackathon grand winnerOpentrons OT-2MuJoCoHarbor

A benchmark that tests whether AI agents can turn the methods sections of published papers into executable liquid-handling robot protocols, aimed at science's reproducibility crisis.

  • 11 expert-annotated tasks, growing to 25 toward publication
  • Graded on wet-lab correctness the simulator doesn't check: mixing, bead carry-over, well volumes
  • Now working with UniteLabs and the NHS to take it to production

Ignis: RL for wildfire suppression

🥇 1st placeGymnasiumNumPyCEM / PPO

A fast fire-spread simulator and RL sandbox, built in 6 hours. An air-tanker agent learns where to drop water. The trained agent saves about 98% of fuel, against 42% with no intervention. Judged by researchers from NVIDIA, Project Prometheus and NASA.

  • 2D wind-driven, 3D terrain (fire climbs uphill) and indoor voxel buildings
  • Pip-installable Gymnasium environments, listed via a PR to Farama's Gymnasium

Benchmarks & research

PallasBench

The first benchmark for LLM-generated JAX Pallas kernels on TPU and GPU. Three difficulty levels, correctness-gated speedup scoring against JAX baselines, robustness filters, CI and a Hugging Face dataset.

JaxBench

KernelBench, except the thing being optimised is JAX itself. Agents rewrite C++/CUDA hot paths and XLA passes, JAX is rebuilt, and the score is the speedup of the real library, gated on correctness.

C++XLAShinkaEvolve

LewyGym

A ProteinGym-compatible benchmark for zero-shot variant effect prediction across the Parkinson's gene panel (SNCA, LRRK2, GBA, PRKN, PINK1…). Finding: ESM-2's signal inverts on SNCA, and larger models make it worse.

protein LMsESM-2Parkinson's

ESM-2 650M inference optimisation

Reproduces the published ProteinGym baseline for ESM-2 650M (ρ = 0.414) on Modal A100s, pinned to exact SHAs. Compares masked-marginal, batched and single-pass scoring, followed by kernel-level work in Mojo, CuteDSL, Pallas and quantisation.
